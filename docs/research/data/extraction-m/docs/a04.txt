As a U.S. citizen traveling to Japan for tourism or short-term business, you generally do **not need a visa** for stays of up to **90 days** under the visa exemption agreement between the two countries.

Key points:
- **Visa-exempt period:** Up to 90 days for tourism, business, or visiting friends/family.
- **Purpose of visit:** Tourism, business meetings, attending conferences, visiting relatives.
- **Passport validity:** Your passport should be valid for the duration of your stay.
- **Return/onward ticket:** You may be required to show proof of onward or return travel.

If you plan to stay longer than 90 days, work, study, or engage in other activities, you will need to apply for the appropriate visa before traveling.

Always check the latest information from the Japanese Embassy or Consulate websites before your trip, as policies can change.
