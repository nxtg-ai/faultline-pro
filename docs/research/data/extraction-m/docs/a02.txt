A common guideline for how much you should have saved for retirement by age 40 in the US is **3 times your annual salary**. This rule of thumb comes from financial experts and institutions like Fidelity and Vanguard. 

Here’s a general framework often recommended:

- By age 30: Aim to have saved about 1x your annual salary.
- By age 40: Aim for about 3x your annual salary.
- By age 50: About 6x your annual salary.
- By age 60: About 8x your annual salary.
- By retirement (around age 67): About 10-12x your annual salary.

For example, if you earn $70,000 per year at 40, ideally you would have saved around $210,000 in retirement accounts.

**Keep in mind:**

- These are general benchmarks and can vary based on your retirement goals, lifestyle, expected expenses, and when you plan to retire.
- It’s important to factor in other retirement sources such as Social Security, pensions, or other investments.
- The cost of living and inflation also affect how much you need to save.
- Personal circumstances like health, family responsibilities, and market performance can affect your savings needs.

If you want a more personalized target, a good next step is to use retirement calculators or speak with a financial advisor to build a plan based on your specific income, expenses, expected retirement age, and lifestyle goals.
