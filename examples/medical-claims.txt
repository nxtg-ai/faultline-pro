A recent meta-analysis found that GPT-4 achieves 87.3% diagnostic accuracy on
standardized clinical vignettes, outperforming the average physician score of
72.1% reported in the same study. The model correctly identified acute
myocardial infarction from ECG descriptions in 94% of cases, though it
misclassified Brugada syndrome as benign early repolarization in 3 of 12
test cases.

Patients treated with the AI-assisted triage protocol at Memorial General
Hospital experienced a 31% reduction in emergency department wait times
during the six-month pilot, with no statistically significant difference in
adverse outcomes compared to the control group (p=0.42). However, the study
was limited to a single site with 1,847 patients and has not been replicated.

The FDA cleared the AI diagnostic tool under the De Novo pathway in January
2025, making it the first LLM-based system authorized for clinical decision
support in radiology. The clearance covers chest X-ray interpretation only
and requires physician oversight for all findings.
